Around mid-June 2026, James Cummings (Newcastle University) undertook a conversion of the Medieval English Theatre 
website from hand-coded HTML markup to a static publishing on GitHub.io. The new website uses Jekyll for publishing 
with Markdown for page authoring. The theme is based on the Minima format with some additions. For example, the 
volume numbering of journal issue pages enables a 'Previous' and 'Next' browsing through the journal. Some other 
standard aspects, such as blockquoting with the '> symbol are hijacked to create an attention drawing highlight box.
